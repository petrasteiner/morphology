NAME
    <GNcompoundswithCELEXRDF> Takes xml-files of GermaNet and extracts trees
    structures of the compounds. Many options can be made, e.g. restricting
    the trees to a certain depth of analysis. Optionally, these analyses can
    be combined with the analyses of the refurbished CELEX database for
    German. Also here, many options can be made, e.g. skip conversions or
    restrict the trees to a certain depth of analysis.
